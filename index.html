Our Legacy

Texprint is a proprietorship concern established in the year 1990 by a visionary woman entrepreneur. What started as a local initiative has grown into a renowned manufacturing and processing unit for home furnishing items.

Our brand name 'Texprint' has become a household name, synonymous with quality and trust. We market our products throughout the country, with a deep-rooted presence in every corner of West Bengal.

A Note from the Founder

"When I started Texprint in 1990, my vision was simple: to bring the warmth of authentic craftsmanship into every home.
